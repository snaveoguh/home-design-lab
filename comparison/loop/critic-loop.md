Read all nine images with the Read tool before writing. You do not know who made them and must not guess.

Locked constraints (not findings): near-ink text, off-white canvas, white panels with hairlines, Base blue single primary accent, no gradients/glass/shadows, green up / red down, no compliance copy.

Output:
1. A correctness table (severity blocker / major / minor · entry · element · finding).
2. A ranked order 1–3 with two sentences each. Ties are allowed only if you genuinely cannot separate two entries; if you tie, say what would break the tie.
3. One paragraph: is the gap between first and last a matter of craft or of product decisions, and which entries would you put in front of a client as-is.
