# Critique · Home (funded) · build B2

Reviewed against `money-ui-critique`. Renders: B-funded, B-loading, B-pending at 390×844 CSS px (2x). All coordinates below are CSS px (render px ÷ 2). Locked constraints (near-ink text, off-white canvas, white panels with hairlines, Base blue single accent, no gradients/glass/shadows, green up / red down, no compliance copy) were checked and all hold; none of them appears as a finding.

## Keep unchanged

1. **Hero and CTA hierarchy.** "Net position" muted label, 45 px-ink balance in tabular figures (the "1" is centred in a full-width digit cell, confirmed by glyph spacing), then one blue "Add money" and one white hairline "Move to savings", both 52 px tall on a 20 px gutter. Blue count is two (button + active Money tab), which is exactly the rubric's limit. Do not add a third blue thing.
2. **Positions panel.** White panel, 1 px hairlines, 60 px rows, 40 px media circles, amounts right-aligned on a shared edge at x 325 with the secondary line ("4.04% APY", "Verified") under the amount, and the Debt row as a verified zero ("$0.00 / Verified", "Nothing owed"). This is the correct empty-verified pattern; keep the muted amount on the zero.
3. **Activity signing and state colour.** "+$250.00" in green with a green media circle for money in, plain ink for the internal "Saved" moves, amber reserved for the one pending item, chevrons and "See all" in ink rather than blue. Semantic colour is only on state, as the rubric wants.

## Verdict

Not ready for review. The funded and pending states are structurally sound; the loading state is broken (sideways overflow, missing tab bar) and the funded screen repeats its two secondary figures. Fix the blocker and the three majors, then the geometry and hairline minors, and re-render all three states with the mechanical check.
